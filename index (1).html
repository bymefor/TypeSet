Free Online Typing Speed Test — Practice Typing and Improve Your WPM

Typeset is a free typing speed test and typing practice tool that measures your words per minute (WPM), accuracy, and consistency in real time. Whether you're a student learning to touch type, a professional wanting faster keyboarding for work, or a writer chasing a personal best, Typeset gives you an instant, no-signup way to test and improve your typing speed.

Typing test modes

  • Timed test — classic 15, 30, 60, or 120 second typing speed tests with randomly generated common-word passages, plus a fully custom timer (any duration you choose) and an untimed option for pressure-free warm-ups.
  • Articles mode — practice typing with a pool of 100+ auto-generated, news-style article passages across business, technology, science, sports, health, and more, so sessions stay varied instead of repeating the same copy.
  • Custom mode — paste any text or upload your own .txt file (an assignment, a work document, a favorite paragraph) and turn it into a typing test. Save articles you use often so you can practice the same passage again later, entirely from your own browser.

Why use Typeset to practice typing

Typeset tracks your typing speed test results over time, including your best WPM, current streak, and total characters typed, so you can watch your typing skills improve session after session. Mechanical keyboard sound effects, a focus mode that hides distractions, a custom timer, and light/dark themes make each typing practice session feel like using a real newsroom typewriter — while Custom mode lets you train on the exact material you actually need to type faster.

Who typing practice helps

  • Students preparing for school typing tests and keyboarding class benchmarks
  • Job seekers who need to list an accurate WPM on their resume
  • Writers, developers, and data-entry professionals who type for a living
  • Anyone learning touch typing or working to reduce typos and increase accuracy
  • Anyone who wants to practice typing their own script, essay, or work document with Custom mode

Frequently asked questions

What is a good typing speed (WPM)?

The average typing speed is around 40 WPM. Professional typists often reach 65-75 WPM, and competitive typists can exceed 120 WPM. Use the timed test on Typeset regularly to track your progress toward these benchmarks.

How is WPM calculated?

Words per minute is calculated by dividing the number of correctly typed characters by 5 (the average word length), then dividing by the elapsed time in minutes. Typeset calculates this live as you type.

Is Typeset free to use?

Yes. Typeset's typing speed test and all typing practice modes are completely free, with no account or sign-up required.

Does Typeset save my typing test history?

Typeset stores your best WPM, streak, and total characters typed locally in your browser, so your progress is remembered the next time you visit on the same device.

Can I type my own text or upload my own article?

Yes — switch to Custom mode, paste any text or upload a .txt file, then start typing. You can also name and save articles you use often so they're ready to practice again next time, without needing an account.

Can I set my own custom typing test duration?

Yes. In Timed mode, click the Custom chip to enter any duration in seconds, or choose Untimed for relaxed, pressure-free practice with no countdown.

Does Articles mode repeat the same passages?

Articles mode draws from over 100 generated passage variations across categories like business, technology, science, and sports, and skips your most recently seen passages so back-to-back sessions feel fresh.
