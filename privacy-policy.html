Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Typeset ("we", "us") collects when you use typeset.example (the "Site"), and how it's used. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information stored on your device

Typeset uses your browser's local storage to save your typing statistics — words per minute, accuracy, streaks, and personal bests — directly on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers and is not linked to your identity. Clearing your browser data will remove it.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

The Site itself does not set first-party tracking cookies. However, third-party services we use — including advertising and analytics providers — may set cookies or use similar technologies (such as web beacons or local storage) in your browser to serve ads and measure their performance.

3. Advertising and Google AdSense

Typeset displays advertising served by Google AdSense and its partners. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Analytics

We may use privacy-conscious analytics tools to understand aggregate traffic patterns — for example, how many people visit the Site and which pages or modes are used most. Analytics data is aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors.

5. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require account registration and do not collect names, email addresses, or passwords to use the typing test.
  • We do not store the text you type beyond what's needed to score the current test in your browser session.
  • We do not sell personal information.

6. Children's privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your choices

You can clear locally stored data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain. You can also use browser extensions or settings to block third-party cookies, which will limit ad personalization but not prevent the Site from functioning.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our Contact page.
